How to play

It's the night of 5 December. You're locked in His castle.

Find the four parts of the Silver Crossbow on candlelit pedestals. When it's whole, one silver bolt kills Him. You get three.

Some walls are not walls. Hidden stone panels pivot open into the next room. Only a few work at any moment, and which ones keeps changing. Look for hairline cracks and dust drifting on a cold draft. He cannot follow you through them.

There is one way out: an old door in a sealed room deep in the castle, reachable only through a hidden passage. Forcing it open takes time and is very, very loud. He will hear it.

His helpers are harmless. Don't touch them.

He sees your flashlight from far away. Turn it off and crouch to hide. If He catches your friend, find the sack and cut them down.
